A round, start to finish

Stake. Play. Verified. Paid.

Deposit

Pay a Lightning invoice. Your sats arrive in the browser wallet as e-cash of a federation.

Stake

Join a round. Your stake is locked in the federation, not paid to an operator.

Verify

The round's committee of nodes replays the runs or checks the signed price. 5 of 7 must sign the same result.

Paid

Winnings land back in your wallet. Withdraw to any Lightning wallet.

Why it can be trusted

Nobody to trust with your sats.

01No central server

Rounds run on independent nodes. Any bonded operator can host them; no company runs the game.

02No custodian

Your stake is locked in a federation, not paid to an operator. No single party can take it.

03Every result checked

Nodes replay every run. A result counts only when 5 of the round's 7 seats sign the same one, and anyone can object for 10 minutes.

04Found on Bitcoin

Every node posts a bond and a signed advertisement on Bitcoin. Your browser finds the nodes there and checks them itself.

Log in with Nostr

An nsec, a NIP-07 browser extension, or a NIP-46 remote signer: a bunker link or a QR code for your phone signer.

Your wallet follows you

Your game wallet's seed is kept on Nostr, NIP-44 encrypted to your own key (NIP-78). Log in anywhere and it is back.

Nostr Wallet Connect

Connect your Lightning wallet once (NIP-47) to pay deposits and stakes, and to withdraw winnings into it.

Run a node with your key

Node owners claim and manage their node with a Nostr key. With an extension or remote signer, every owner action is a signed NIP-98 event (kind 27235) the node checks.

The Arena relay

relay.bitcoin-arena.io keeps profiles, relay lists and encrypted seeds, and carries remote signing and wallet requests. Nothing else.

Community on Nostr

Under the hood

Built on Bitcoin, end to end.

Bonds

Every node posts a bond on Bitcoin. No bond, no seat.

Committees

Each round's committee is drawn from the bonded nodes by a Bitcoin block hash.

Replay

Games are deterministic. Nodes re-run every input and sign the result.

Custody

Stakes lock in a Fedimint federation whose guardians are the nodes, never in one company's wallet.

Settlement

Payouts and withdrawals move as sats over Lightning.
